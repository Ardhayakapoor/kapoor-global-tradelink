Who We Are

Trade Connections That Create Opportunity

Kapoor Global TradeLink is an FMCG trading and procurement business based in Chandigarh, India, focused on connecting buyers with suitable suppliers across multiple product categories.

We coordinate order-based sourcing, commercial discussions, supplier communication and export-related requirements according to buyer specifications and product availability.

With a focus on India and the UAE, including Dubai, we aim to build dependable business relationships through transparent communication and responsible trade coordination.
