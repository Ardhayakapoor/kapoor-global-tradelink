Who We Are

A Thoughtful Approach to Trading and Sourcing

Kapoor Global TradeLink operates in the field of FMCG trading, bulk sourcing, procurement and export coordination. Based in Chandigarh, India, the company focuses on understanding buyer requirements and coordinating suitable sourcing solutions.

Our business approach is built around clear communication, requirement-based coordination and practical trade support. We aim to make sourcing conversations straightforward and professional for businesses operating in India and the UAE.

Product specifications, packaging, minimum order quantities, availability, pricing and delivery arrangements are confirmed according to each individual enquiry and order.
