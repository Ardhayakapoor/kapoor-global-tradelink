Who We Are

Trade Relationships Built Around Your Requirements

Kapoor Global TradeLink is a Chandigarh-based B2B trading and export coordination business focused on bulk sourcing and supply of FMCG products.

We work to identify suitable agricultural cooperatives, food processing units, manufacturers and established suppliers across India to fulfil domestic and international buyer requirements.

Our flexible, order-based sourcing model allows procurement to be coordinated according to product specifications, pricing, availability and buyer demand.
