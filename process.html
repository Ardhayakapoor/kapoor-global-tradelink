How We Work

A Clear Approach to Sourcing and Trade Coordination

Every business requirement is different. Our approach begins with understanding the enquiry and coordinating the relevant sourcing and order-related discussions.

Our Working Approach

From Business Enquiry to Order Coordination

We follow a requirement-led approach designed to keep business discussions organised and expectations clear. The stages below describe our general coordination process and may vary depending on the product, supplier, destination and order requirements.

01

Understanding Requirements

We begin by understanding the buyer's product category, specifications, required quantity, packaging preferences and destination.

02

Sourcing Enquiry

Based on the requirement, we coordinate sourcing enquiries and explore suitable product and supplier possibilities.

03

Quotation and Discussion

Relevant details such as product specifications, pricing, minimum order quantities, packaging and estimated timelines are discussed for confirmation.

04

Order Confirmation

Once the commercial terms and order details are agreed, the confirmed requirements and responsibilities are documented before proceeding.

05

Order Coordination

We coordinate relevant order-related communication with the concerned parties according to the agreed arrangement.

06

Dispatch and Follow-Up

Dispatch arrangements, applicable documentation and delivery-related updates are coordinated according to the confirmed order terms and responsibilities.

Important: Product availability, supplier confirmation, pricing, documentation, shipping arrangements and delivery timelines are subject to individual enquiry, final agreement and the responsibilities accepted by the relevant parties.
