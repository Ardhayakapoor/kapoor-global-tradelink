About Kapoor Global TradeLink

Building Connections Through Trade

A business focused on FMCG trading, bulk sourcing, procurement and export coordination, with a commitment to clear communication and requirement-led business relationships.

Our Company

Understanding Trade Beyond Transactions

Kapoor Global TradeLink is based in Chandigarh, India, and operates in FMCG trading, bulk sourcing, procurement and export coordination.

We focus on understanding the specific requirements of buyers and coordinating sourcing opportunities according to product category, quantity, specifications and destination market.

Our approach is built around professional communication, practical coordination and clarity at every stage of a business enquiry.

We work with a focus on India and UAE market requirements. Each enquiry is considered individually, with product availability, pricing, packaging, minimum order quantities and delivery arrangements confirmed before an order is finalised.

Our Approach

Trade Begins With Understanding the Right Requirement

We believe that good business coordination starts with listening, clear expectations and transparent communication. Our focus is to connect buyer requirements with suitable sourcing possibilities.
